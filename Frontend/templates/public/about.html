About and help

How Konektya works

Konektya brings reliable health information, healthcare facilities, services and emergency contacts in Kenya together in one place, so you spend less time checking many different sources.

Using Konektya

  1. Search. Type a word such as a condition, town or service into the search box on the home page or the search page. Select a result to read it in full.
  2. Read health information. Open Health information, choose a topic, then choose a page. Each page shows its source and when it was last reviewed.
  3. Find a facility. Open Facilities and search by name, town, county or service. Select a facility to see its location, services and contact details.
  4. Find a service. Open Services and choose one, such as emergency care or HIV testing, to see which facilities list it.
  5. Get help in an emergency. Call 999 or 112, or open Emergency contacts for more verified numbers. On a phone, tap a number to call it.

What Konektya does not do

  • It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe medicine or give personal medical advice.
  • It does not replace doctors, nurses, clinical officers, pharmacists or other health workers.
  • It does not book appointments or connect to hospital or patient records.
  • It does not provide emergency services. In an emergency, call 999 or 112.

Where the information comes from

  • Health information is based on credible sources, such as the World Health Organization, and every page names its source.
  • Emergency numbers are checked against official sources before they are published, and the date they were verified is shown.
  • Facility details come from official or government sources. Anything that could not be confirmed is marked “Not available”.
  • Details can change. Call a facility before you travel to confirm opening hours and services.

Your privacy

  • You do not need an account to use Konektya, and searches are not saved.
  • If you choose to create an account, Konektya stores only your username, email address and a protected (hashed) password.
  • Konektya never asks for your medical history.

General information, not medical advice

If you are unwell or worried about your health, speak to a health worker. If someone's life is in danger, call 999 or 112 now.
