Administrator guide

How to keep Konektya's information accurate and up to date.

Signing in and out

  • Open the home page, select System and log in with your username or email and password.
  • After five failed attempts, login is paused for 15 minutes.
  • Select Log out in the grey bar at the top of any page when you finish.
  • New administrators are added on the server with flask create-admin.

Health information

  1. Go to Add health information. Choose a category and enter a title, summary and the page text.
  2. Add the source name and link. A page cannot be published without a source.
  3. Save. New pages are saved as drafts and open in a preview.
  4. Check the preview, then select Publish. You can return a page to draft at any time.

Formatting: leave a blank line between paragraphs, start a line with ## for a sub-heading and - for a bullet point.

Facilities and services

  • Add or edit facilities under Facilities. Leave a field empty if it cannot be confirmed; the public site shows it as “Not available”.
  • Tick the services each facility offers. Manage the service list under Services.
  • Record the source and the date you last checked the details.

Emergency contacts

  • Only add a number after checking it against an official source.
  • Every contact needs a source and the date it was verified. Use the display order to put the most important numbers first.

Deleting content

  • Select Delete next to a record and confirm. This cannot be undone.
  • A health category that still contains pages cannot be deleted. Move or delete its pages first.

Records, backups and reviews

  • Every login, change, publish, backup and restore is listed in the Activity log.
  • Download a backup from Backup and restore before large changes, and keep it private.
  • Review health pages, facility details and emergency numbers regularly and update the reviewed dates.
