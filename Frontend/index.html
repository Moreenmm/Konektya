Our commitment

Information you can trust

Konektya is built to help you reach care, not to replace it.

  • Sourced

    Every health page names the credible source it is based on, such as the World Health Organization.

  • Verified

    Emergency numbers are checked against official sources and show the date they were verified.

  • Reviewed

    Facility details and health pages show when they were last reviewed. Unconfirmed details are marked “Not available”.

  • Private

    You do not need an account to use Konektya, and your searches are never saved.
